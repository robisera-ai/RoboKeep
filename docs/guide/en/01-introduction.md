# Introduction

**RoboKeep turns `robocopy` — the copy engine already built into every Windows PC — into a real
backup tool**, with a simple interface in place of the command line.

The core idea is this: robocopy is powerful and dependable, but awkward to run by hand and to
configure. RoboKeep puts a window in front of it where you set up your backups once, start them
with a click, and see them at a glance — without giving up anything robocopy can do.

## What it can do

- **Copy and mirror** your folders, keeping the destination identical to the source (or only
  adding and updating, never deleting, if you prefer).
- **Dated versions** of your files, like a time machine: recover how a document looked last week.
- **Copy open files**, even ones locked by other programs.
- **Integrity verification**: mathematical proof the copy is intact.
- **Scheduling**: each backup runs on its own, at the time you choose, even with the app closed.
- **Rotating-disk protection**: if you alternate several external disks, RoboKeep never writes to
  the wrong one.

## Who it's for

For anyone who wants serious **local** backups, under their own control: no cloud, no account, no
subscription. Your files stay on your disks, full stop.

You don't need to know robocopy: a **guided setup** asks you a few questions in plain language and
configures the backup for you. But if you're an expert, every single option is there, adjustable
by hand, with a preview of the exact command always in view.
